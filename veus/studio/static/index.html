01 / Vehicle & road

Controller & lead vehicle

ACC uses a constant-speed lead vehicle. AEB starts with cruise control, then latches emergency braking on collision risk. These are educational algorithms, not production safety systems.

Battery & energy settings

Capacity is stored chemical energy. Positive battery power means discharge; negative means charging.

Settings apply on reset. Presets define pedal commands; speed is calculated from forces.
